Privacy Policy

Effective January 1, 2025

Auto Fury LLC respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you visit our website, request a quote, or communicate with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle year, make, and model, transport preference, and the contents of messages you send us.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited, and general usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the website.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Prepare and deliver vehicle transport quotes.
  • Coordinate pickup, transport, delivery, and customer support.
  • Respond to questions and service requests.
  • Send transactional or service-related communications.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

SMS and Mobile Information

If you opt in to receive text messages from Auto Fury LLC, we may use your mobile number to send quote updates, scheduling information, transport status updates, and customer service messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary with service providers that help us operate our business, process requests, coordinate vehicle transportation, maintain our website, or comply with law. These providers may use information only to perform services for us and are expected to protect it.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights or safety, or in connection with a business reorganization. SMS opt-in data and consent are excluded from these sharing categories and are not shared with third parties.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, and operational requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update or delete personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may unsubscribe from text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Auto Fury LLC at (847) 773-0062, email quotes@autofuryllc.com, or use our contact form.
