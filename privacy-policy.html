Privacy Policy

Effective January 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Auto Fury LLC collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information received from visitors to this website. It applies to the website and to the vehicle transportation services offered through it.

Personal Identification Information

We may collect personal information when you visit the website, request a quote, contact us, arrange vehicle transportation, or use other features and services available through the website. Depending on how you interact with us, this information may include your name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery ZIP codes, vehicle year, make and model, transport preference, and the contents of messages you send.

You may browse the website without submitting personal information. We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it. You may decline to provide information, although doing so may prevent us from preparing a quote, responding to a request, or providing certain services.

Non-Personal Identification Information

We may collect non-personal or technical information when you interact with the website. This may include browser type, device type, operating system, internet service provider, IP address, pages visited, referring pages, and similar information about how you connect to and use the website.

Web Browser Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar browser technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand general usage, and improve the user experience. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies or notify you when cookies are being sent. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

How We Use Collected Information

Auto Fury LLC may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide customer service: Information you provide helps us respond to quote requests, questions, scheduling needs, and support requests.
  • To provide transportation services: We use route, vehicle, and contact information to prepare quotes and coordinate pickup, transport, and delivery.
  • To improve our website and services: We may use feedback and aggregate usage information to understand how visitors use the website and to improve our services.
  • To communicate with you: We may use your email address or phone number to respond to inquiries and send transactional or service-related updates.
  • To protect our operations: We may use information to maintain website security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

SMS and Mobile Information

If you opt in to receive text messages from Auto Fury LLC, we may use your mobile number to send quote updates, scheduling information, transport status updates, and customer service messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with transportation providers and other service providers only when reasonably necessary to prepare or fulfill your requested vehicle transport, operate the website, process communications, provide customer support, or comply with law. These providers are permitted to use the information only for the services they perform.

We may also disclose information when required by law, when necessary to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction. We may share aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify an individual.

SMS opt-in data and consent are excluded from all sharing categories described above and are not shared with third parties.

Data Retention and Your Choices

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services and satisfy legal, accounting, and operational requirements. You may ask us to review, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions. You may stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is important to us. The website and our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect or maintain personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date displayed at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect the information we collect.

Your Acceptance of This Policy

By using this website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the website or submit personal information through it. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy.

Contacting Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our privacy practices, or your interactions with this website, contact Auto Fury LLC at (847) 773-0062, email quotes@autofuryllc.com, or use our contact form.
